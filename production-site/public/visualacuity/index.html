The chart, within reach

Built for the way
you screen.

Choose a chart, adjust the presentation and screen one eye at a time. A simple interface keeps the chart at the center of the experience.

01 / CHARTS

Sloan letters and more

Five-letter Sloan lines, a full Sloan chart with a movable row pointer, crowded Sloan letters, tumbling E, Landolt C and pediatric symbols. Tap to shuffle and swipe to change size or chart.

02 / DISTANCE

Sizing that follows distance

On a supported iPhone, the TrueDepth front camera estimates eye-to-screen distance so chart symbols resize as you move. Available sizes depend on the display, distance and tracking quality.

03 / GUIDANCE

Optional voice testing

Guided mode presents letters one at a time and recognizes spoken answers in English or Spanish on the iPhone. Review saved sessions in the log and choose whether to share a report.

Before you begin

Device &
permissions.

See setup and troubleshooting ↗
  • iOS 17 or later.
  • Automatic distance sizing and guided testing require an iPhone with a TrueDepth front camera and camera permission.
  • Voice mode also needs microphone and speech-recognition permission, with on-device English or Spanish recognition available.
  • Manual charts remain available when camera or voice permissions are declined. A manual chart without distance tracking does not provide automatic distance-based sizing.

Use the app in portrait orientation with an unobstructed front camera and enough light for face tracking. The support page explains chart controls and common setup issues.

Privacy, by design

On your iPhone.
Under your control.

Camera frames and speech are processed on the device. The app has no account, advertising or analytics. Saved screening sessions stay in app storage unless you choose to export or share them.
