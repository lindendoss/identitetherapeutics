The essentials

Getting started

  1. Open a chart. Swipe up or down to switch charts. Swipe left or right to change the letter size or move the full-chart row pointer. Tap the chart to shuffle.
  2. Allow camera access for distance sizing. Use a TrueDepth-equipped iPhone, hold it in portrait and keep the front camera unobstructed in a well-lit room. Tap the distance readout to switch units.
  3. Use voice mode if you choose. Tap the microphone button and allow microphone and speech recognition. Follow the guide to cover one eye and speak each letter aloud in English or Spanish.
  4. Review your session. Open the log using the book icon to review saved results and available sharing options. The ? button opens the app’s instructions and screening statement.

Troubleshooting

Common questions

Which charts and controls are available?

The app includes a five-letter Sloan line, a full Sloan chart, crowded Sloan letters, tumbling E, Landolt C and pediatric symbols. Swipe up or down to change charts. Swipe left or right to change size or move the full-chart pointer; tap to shuffle. Tap the distance readout to switch between feet and inches and centimeters.

Why is the distance readout blank or showing a dash?

Automatic sizing requires an iPhone with a TrueDepth front camera and camera permission. On an unsupported device, or when tracking is unavailable, the manual charts remain visible but automatic distance-based sizing is unavailable.

Check camera access in iPhone Settings, keep the app in portrait, uncover the front camera and improve the room lighting. Hold the phone in front of your face. A simulator does not provide TrueDepth tracking.

Can I decline camera or microphone access?

Yes. The manual charts remain available when permissions are declined. Camera access is needed for automatic distance sizing and guided testing. Microphone and speech-recognition access are needed for voice mode. You can review these permissions in iPhone Settings.

How do I start guided voice testing?

On a supported TrueDepth iPhone, tap the microphone button and follow the guide. Hold a hand flat over one eye, close to that side of your face. The app selects the other eye and presents one letter at a time. Say the letter aloud, or say “I can’t see it” when you cannot read it. Follow the prompts to screen the other eye.

Guided testing requires camera permission. Voice mode also needs microphone and speech-recognition permission and on-device recognition for your selected English or Spanish language.

Why is voice mode not recognizing my answers?

Check microphone and speech-recognition permissions, confirm that your selected English or Spanish language supports on-device recognition, and try a quieter room. Speak clearly and at a comfortable volume. If a headset is connected, check which microphone it is using.

Silence does not advance the screening. The app offers the same letter again. Manual charts are available when voice recognition is unavailable.

What do the green and red letters mean?

In guided voice mode, a recognized correct response appears green and an incorrect response appears red. The color remains until the next letter is ready. These colors describe the app’s response scoring; they are not a diagnosis or an indication of eye health.

How do I review, delete or share saved results?

Open the log using the book icon to review dated right- and left-eye sessions. Use the log’s delete controls to remove saved sessions. Use the report’s share action to send it through the iPhone share sheet or save it as a file.

The app does not send results to DOSSMD automatically. If you choose to share, the recipient and destination you select will receive the report. Review the contents first and follow the privacy requirements of your clinical setting.

What information is in a diagnostics report?

An optional diagnostics export contains technical information such as app version and build, iOS version, generic device family, timing, tracking status and technical logs. It does not include camera images, audio, presented letters or speech transcripts. The report goes only to the destination you choose; review it before sharing.

Is the app free? Do I need an account?

DOSS Visual Acuity is free, with no advertising and no account or sign-in. The app does not include analytics. See the privacy policy for camera, voice, stored-session and support-email details.

Can I use a result to make a medical decision?

DOSS Visual Acuity is for screening only. It is not for diagnosis or medical management and does not replace a comprehensive eye examination. Discuss symptoms or concerns with an ophthalmologist or another qualified eye-care professional.

Screening is one part of eye care.

This app and its support email do not provide medical advice or urgent care. Consult an ophthalmologist or other qualified eye-care professional before making medical decisions. For sudden loss of vision or other urgent eye symptoms, seek prompt medical care.
