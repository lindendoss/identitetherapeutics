Overview

The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or developer-operated servers. It does not automatically send us your personal information, camera data, speech or screening results.

This policy describes the app’s on-device processing and the information you may choose to share. It also explains what happens if you email support or visit these app information pages.

Camera and face data

With your permission, the app uses the front camera and the TrueDepth camera’s ARKit face tracking to estimate the distance from your eyes to the screen and, in guided mode, which eye you have covered with your hand. Processing happens on your iPhone in real time.

The app does not take photographs or video, save camera frames or face geometry, identify anyone, or send camera frames or face geometry off the device. Face data is not used for advertising or marketing.

Saved screening sessions may include summaries of viewing distance and response timing. These are stored with the session as described below; they are not saved camera images or a face model.

You can turn camera access off in iPhone Settings. Manual charts remain available, while automatic distance sizing and guided testing require camera access and a supported TrueDepth iPhone.

Microphone and speech

With your permission, voice mode recognizes spoken letters and color-plate numbers using Apple’s on-device speech recognition in English or Spanish. The app also measures background sound level to prepare voice mode.

Audio and speech transcripts are processed on your iPhone. The app does not save or transmit audio or transcripts. Microphone and speech-recognition permissions can be changed in iPhone Settings; manual charts remain available when voice permissions are declined.

Screening results and app storage

Guided-session results are stored in the app on your iPhone. A saved session may include its date, eye, screening result, viewing-distance summaries and response timing. The app also stores preferences such as your selected chart and language on the device.

You can review and delete saved sessions in the app’s log. You can choose to export or share a report using the iPhone share sheet. We do not receive results automatically.

When you choose to share or save a report outside the app, the destination you select controls that copy under its own privacy practices. Deleting a session from the app does not delete copies you previously exported or shared.

Optional diagnostics

If you choose to export a diagnostics report, it contains limited technical information, including app version and build, iOS version, generic device family, timing, tracking status and technical logs. It does not include camera images, audio, presented letters or speech transcripts.

The report goes only to the destination you select. We receive it only if you choose to send it to us, for example with a support email. You may review the report before sharing it.

Children

The app is intended for clinicians and trainees. It does not knowingly collect personal information automatically from anyone, including children. Use of a pediatric chart does not create an account or send information to us.

Support emails and this website

If you email info@identitetherapeutics.com, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request and address app issues. Support correspondence is handled through our email provider and may be retained as part of that correspondence.

Please do not send patient names, identifying information or clinical records. You can contact us to ask about information you have sent in a support message.

These app information, support and privacy pages do not use analytics, advertising trackers, forms or cookies. The website’s hosting provider may process standard technical request information, such as IP address and browser details, to deliver and operate the pages. This website hosting activity is separate from the app’s on-device screening functions.
